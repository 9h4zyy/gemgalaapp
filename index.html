For the social spark

Become a Host

Hosts participate through livestreams, party rooms, mini-games, and other host activities—ideal if you enjoy connecting with an audience.

Host benefits

  • Meet users from around the world.
  • Build a personal audience in the app.
  • Receive gifts that may be withdrawn.
  • Complete eligible tasks for potential weekly rewards.

Host earnings

  • Gift earnings from livestreams, party rooms, and mini-games.
  • Daily task rewards may be available to qualified or active Hosts.

For the team builder

Become an Agent

Agents focus on inviting new users and developing a team, with opportunities through the Gemgala agency system.

Agent benefits

  • Grow a team on an active platform.
  • Access better earning opportunities as you level up.
  • Explore additional coin seller programs.

Agent earnings

  • Invitation rewards for successfully referred users.
  • Commission opportunities based on team earnings.
  • Coin seller commissions for eligible Agents.

Withdrawal requirements

  • Link a MetWallet account to Gemgala.
  • Meet the current minimum: $10 or 100,000 Gems.
  • Your account must not be suspended.

Minimums and available options may change. Follow the latest instructions in the app.
