Gemgala App Host & Agent

What is a Host?

A Gemgala Host is a user who participates in the platform through livestreams, party, mini games, and host-related activities. This is designed for users who enjoy engaging with an audience.

Host Benefits

  • Meet & engage with users from all over the world.
  • Build a personal audience inside the Gemgala App.
  • Receive gifts that can be withdrawn.
  • Easy to complete tasks for up to hundreds dollar weekly

Host Earnings

  • Gift earnings from livestream, party-room, and gems from mini-games
  • Daily-task rewards available to qualified or active Hosts.

What is a Gemgala Agent?

A Gemgala Agent focuses less on content creation, and more on building a team. Agents invite new users, develop a team, and may earn through the Gemgala agency system.

Gemgala Agent Benefits

  • Easily grow a team on an active Gemgala platform.
  • Access better earning opportunities as you level up.
  • Additional coin seller programs for even more rewards

Agent Earnings

  • Invitation rewards for successfully referred users
  • Commission opportunities based on the team earnings.
  • Coin seller commission opportunities for agents who’s eligible

Gemgala Withdrawal Requirements

  • Link a MetWallet account to your Gemgala account.
  • Meet the current minimum withdrawal amount of $10 or 100,000 Gems
  • Account is not in suspension

Frequently Asked Questions

The Gemgala App is a social entertainment app built around chat, voice party rooms, interactive games, gifts, and community features. Users can join for entertainment and social interaction, while eligible users can also explore the Host and Agent paths.

You can search for Gemgala on Google Play or use our direct download and registration link.

Install the app, choose an available sign-up method such as Google, email, or phone number, then complete the requested profile information. Some features may require additional verification when you use them.

Gemgala provides different participation paths. Hosts may receive gifts and task rewards, while Agents may earn through invitations, team earning, and applicable commission programs. Earnings vary and are not guaranteed.

Gemgala uses MetWallet. Link your wallet, meet the minimum withdrawal requirement, open the Gems menu, select Withdraw, complete email verification, and authorize the connection. Always follow the latest instructions shown in the app.
